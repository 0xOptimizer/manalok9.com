Who We Are

Our History

Started by Dr. Abel Manalo as a dog breeding hobby in 1996 while studying veterinary medicine at UPLB. Originally registered under the PCCI kennel name ACME, it officially became a business named Manalo Pet Shop on July 31, 2007.

Since then, Manalo K9 has evolved into an international company providing world-class working K9 teams and Superdogs all over the Philippines, and in conflict zones such as Iraq, Afghanistan, and Lebanon, as well as the USA.

Beyond deployment, we launched our consumer products brand Superdog in 2010 and the renowned Super Dog Nutrition (SDN) in 2016.

Mission

To provide advanced, superior quality animal products and services and create innovative and effective solutions for those who raise and care for animals.

Vision

To be the global tech leader in animal products and services solutions.
